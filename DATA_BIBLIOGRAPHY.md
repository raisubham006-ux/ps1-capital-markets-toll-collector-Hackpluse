## 3. Market Pricing & Macroeconomic Data Sources

* **National Stock Exchange of India (NSE):** Historical daily Adjusted Closing Prices, Volume-Weighted Average Price (VWAP), 30-day Average Daily Volume (ADV), and delivery percentage data.
* **Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE):** Capitalization tables, institutional shareholding patterns, and historical price bands.
* **Bloomberg / Refinitiv / Trendlyne:** Historical forward P/E multiples, EV/EBITDA bands, and 3-year beta calculations.
